Pattern worth acting on

Only five of the 30 companies have a person whose title actually says "Head of AI" or equivalent: Ringg, Leena, Spyne, CodeRabbit (VP of AI), Neuron7. Everywhere else the technical authority is a founder. Stop hunting for a VP who doesn't exist — pitch founders directly.

The honest limitation

LinkedIn blocks automated access. Nothing here was scraped from LinkedIn itself. Every URL below was literally seen in a public search result or page — none were constructed from a name. That means coverage is good but not complete, and titles can lag reality by 6–12 months. Check the profile before you send anything that states someone's title.

Why coverage stops where it does

  • LinkedIn can't be queried directly. Everything here came from public search results that happened to surface a profile. That systematically over-represents founders and under-represents ICs and mid-level leadership.
  • Most enrichment sites were blocked by the network proxy — TheOrg, Wellfound, RocketReach, Tracxn, Crunchbase, plus nearly every company's own site. Team pages would have closed several of the gaps above.
  • Titles lag. Aggregator data can be a year stale. Four confirmed departures are listed above; assume there are more I didn't catch.

If you want to go deeper

The single highest-leverage upgrade is LinkedIn Sales Navigator (~₹5–6k/month). It would close every "no URL found" above, surface the IC layer that search engines never index, and let you filter by "joined in the last 90 days" — which is the strongest buying signal in this whole exercise. Everything in this document is the free-tier ceiling.

One structural finding to carry into the pitch

Across 30 companies, only five have a dedicated Head of AI. In the other 25, the person who would own "does our agent behave correctly" is a founder who is also doing six other jobs. That is not a gap in your prospect list — it is the reason the product exists, and it belongs in your opening line.
